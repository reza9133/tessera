Lumen keeps your notes with you, everywhere.

Lumen is a notes app for people who think in fragments. Open it, type, and the thought is saved. There is nothing to organise first and nothing to lose.

Capture in a second

A single shortcut opens a blank page. Write a line, a list or a whole idea. Lumen saves every keystroke as you go, so a dead battery or a closed laptop never costs you a sentence.

Find anything instantly

Search reads every note as you type and shows the matching lines, not just titles. Looking for that train time from last spring? Type two words and it is on screen before you finish the third.

Works in offline mode, syncs when you land

On a plane, in a tunnel or in a cabin with no signal, Lumen runs in offline mode and behaves exactly as usual. The moment a connection returns, syncing quietly brings every device up to date, with no buttons to press and no conflicts to untangle.

Start today

Install Lumen on your phone and your laptop, write one note, and see it appear on both. It takes less time than reading this page. Your first thousand notes are free, and they stay yours.
